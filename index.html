UnderKitby WorkinWithAI

Have a melody but not the chords? Sing, hum or play it, and UnderKit finds the chords that go under it, bar by bar. It shows how well each chord fits your notes and how sure it is. Swap or lock any chord, then hear it on a real band.

iPhone/iPad: flip the silent switch off (or use headphones). iOS mutes web audio when the ringer is silenced. Exports open the share sheet: pick "Save to Files".
